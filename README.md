# Retail Sales Performance Analytics

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze retail sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, geographical trends, salesperson performance, and profitability.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project transforms retail sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that helps stakeholders monitor KPIs, identify trends, compare performance, and understand the factors affecting sales and profitability.

The dashboard is organized into multiple analytical pages to provide both a high-level overview and detailed analysis.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Monitor overall sales performance and key business KPIs.
* Analyze sales trends over time.
* Understand customer purchasing behavior.
* Identify top-performing and underperforming products.
* Compare performance across different locations.
* Evaluate salesperson performance.
* Analyze profitability and identify important profit drivers.
* Present business insights through interactive Power BI visualizations.

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Summary

Provides an overview of the most important business KPIs and overall sales performance.

### 2. Customer Analysis

Analyzes customer behavior and purchasing patterns to identify important customer segments and trends.

### 3. Product Analysis

Examines product performance and highlights products contributing significantly to sales.

### 4. Location Analysis

Compares sales performance across different geographical locations.

### 5. Salesperson Analysis

Evaluates salesperson performance and compares individual sales results.

### 6. Profitability Analysis

Provides a deeper analysis of profitability and the factors affecting business performance.
